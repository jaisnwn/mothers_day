Dear Mumma ❤️

I can’t express my feelings openly but I care a lot! I may not be always right but I know I always have your back.

You have always been the strongest person I know. Struggling through many ups and downs, you never collapsed, you had that courage to overcome all the challenges that we’ve faced in the last few years.

Sometimes I may be too rude towards you but I never really have any intentions to hurt you. You’ve been my biggest cheerleader and also my inspiration.

I always look up to you like — “mumma sarkh aplya payavar ubh rahaych ahe!” 🥹

It is really hard for me to stay this long from you but I hope it’s for the best.

Mummy, you always had faith in me that one day I would get the best job and make you proud… and next semester, I finally will! 🤍

I hope I get to fulfill all your wishes you left behind sacrificing for our family! 🥹

Happy Mother’s Day! 💗
